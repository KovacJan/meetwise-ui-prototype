# MeetWise Product Sheet

Version: 1.0  
Audience: End users, team leads, decision makers  
Format: One-pager style content (ready for PDF export)

## MeetWise at a Glance

MeetWise helps teams understand the real cost of meetings and turn calendar data into practical actions.

Instead of guessing where time and budget are lost, teams get clear cost visibility, AI-supported recommendations, and an easier path to better meeting habits.

## Why Teams Use MeetWise

- Too many meetings with unclear value
- Rising collaboration costs without visibility
- Recurring meetings that continue by default
- Limited data for deciding what to keep, shorten, or replace

MeetWise makes these trade-offs visible and actionable.

## Key Benefits

- Cost clarity: See the estimated cost of meetings in real currency terms
- Better decisions: Identify low-value recurring meetings faster
- Time recovery: Reduce unnecessary meeting load across teams
- Practical AI support: Receive clear recommendations, not generic reports
- Fast onboarding: Connect Outlook and start in minutes

## Core Capabilities

### 1) Calendar-Based Meeting Analytics

- Connect Microsoft Outlook Calendar
- Sync meeting metadata securely
- Analyze meeting duration, participants, and frequency

### 2) Meeting Cost Visibility

- Calculate estimated meeting costs from team hourly rates and time spent
- Track cost patterns across week, month, year, and custom periods
- Highlight expensive recurring meetings and trend changes

### 3) AI Insights and Recommendations

- Generate meeting efficiency signals and opportunity areas
- Prioritize recommendations (High, Medium, Low)
- Surface estimated monthly savings potential
- Support team leads with data-backed improvement ideas

### 4) Team-Level Collaboration Features

- Create or join teams
- Invite members and share team code
- Align meeting cost data at team level for shared decision making

### 5) Multi-Language Product Experience

- English and German interface support
- Localized onboarding and product flows

## What Team Leads Can Do with MeetWise

- Track meeting spend trends and identify waste patterns
- Spot over-attended and overlong recurring sessions
- Prioritize optimization actions with AI recommendations
- Support teams with clear, measurable meeting policies
- Monitor whether improvements reduce low-efficiency spend over time

## Typical Workflow

1. Sign up and join or create your team.
2. Connect Outlook Calendar.
3. Sync meetings and review cost baseline.
4. Open AI Insights and prioritize opportunities.
5. Apply changes to recurring meetings.
6. Track cost and efficiency trends over time.
