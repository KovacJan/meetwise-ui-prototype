MeetWise

One-Page Product Sheet

Turn Meeting Time into Measurable Business Value

AI-Powered

MeetWise helps teams understand the real cost of meetings and improve meeting quality with clear analytics and AI-supported recommendations.

What MeetWise Does

1) Connects to Outlook Calendar

  • Secure Microsoft OAuth connection
  • Imports meeting metadata for analysis

2) Calculates Meeting Cost

  • Uses duration, participants, and team hourly rates
  • Tracks trends by week, month, year, and custom range

3) Delivers AI Insights

  • Highlights low-efficiency meeting patterns
  • Prioritizes recommendations (High/Medium/Low)
  • Shows estimated monthly savings opportunities

4) Supports Team Action

  • Gives Team Leads a clear view of total team meeting cost
  • Helps Team Leads manage load, priorities, and optimization actions
  • Supports team-level visibility and alignment

Why AI Matters

MeetWise is not just reporting. It uses AI to detect patterns, rank opportunities, and help Team Leads decide where to act first for maximum meeting impact.

Team Lead Value: Team Leads can monitor team meeting spend, identify waste patterns, and drive measurable optimization decisions with shared, data-backed visibility.

Who It Is For

  • Team leads and managers
  • Operations and department leaders
  • Organizations using Microsoft 365 / Outlook

Key Benefits

  • Better cost visibility across meetings
  • Faster decisions on what to keep, shorten, or remove
  • Reduced low-value meeting time
  • Practical recommendations teams can apply quickly

Typical User Flow

  1. Sign up and create or join a team
  2. Connect Outlook Calendar
  3. Sync and review meeting cost baseline
  4. Open AI Insights and prioritize changes
  5. Track improvement over time

Security and Trust

  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Encrypted handling of sensitive integration tokens
  • Transparent Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
  • Explicit legal consent capture during registration
